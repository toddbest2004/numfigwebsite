Subprocessors

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Numbers & Figures LLC uses the following service providers to operate You Know What I Mean?, provide product analytics, and respond to support requests.

Provider Service and purpose Data processed
Salesforce, Inc. (Heroku) Application hosting, infrastructure, and temporary game-session storage, including Heroku Key-Value Store. Live game and connection information, temporary session state, Discord information required to operate active sessions, and server logs.
PostHog, Inc. Product analytics and technical telemetry. Gameplay and usage events, technical information, and random session-scoped analytics identifiers. Raw Discord user, server, and session identifiers are not provided to PostHog.
Google LLC (Google Workspace) Business email and customer-support communications. Email addresses, message contents, attachments, and other information users choose to provide when contacting us.

These providers are authorized to process data only as necessary to provide their services to Numbers & Figures LLC and are subject to applicable contractual data-protection and security obligations.

We may update this list when our service providers change. Questions about our service providers or privacy practices can be sent to contact@numbersandfigures.com .
